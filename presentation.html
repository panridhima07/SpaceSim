Bridging Desktop Rocket CAD & Interactive Spaceflight

Why student rocketry teams and space enthusiasts need a unified 3D physics sandbox on Web & Mobile.

THE GAPS IN EXISTING TOOLS
01 · DESKTOP-BOUND 2D ROCKET CAD

OpenRocket Cannot Run at the Launch Pad

Student rocket teams design in OpenRocket (.ork), which is locked to desktop Java, offers only static 2D schematics, and cannot be inspected or flown on phones in the field.

02 · OPAQUE FLIGHT FAILURES

Raw CSV Plots Hide Why a Rocket Failed

When a design tumbles, weathercocks, or shreds fins, 2D charts do not show 3D force vectors (CG vs CP) or explain which component caused the instability and how to fix it.

03 · DISCONNECTED ISRO & DEEP-SPACE DATA

Arcade Mobile Sims vs Static ISRO Telemetry

Mobile games simplify orbital physics, while real ISRO mission data (LVM3-M4, Chandrayaan-3, RH-200) sits in static tables instead of an interactive 3D world.

THE ASTRA SPACEFLIGHT SOLUTION
DOMAIN 05 · MOON, MARS & DEEP-SPACE EXPLORATION

Solar-System 3D Sandbox + Lunar Powered Descent

  • True-scale JPL Keplerian Solar System (Sun, 8 planets from Mercury to Neptune, Moon) with d3-celestial star catalog & time warp up to 100,000×.
  • Interactive 6-DOF Powered Lunar Descent from 100 km perilune to touchdown with procedural boulder fields, slope hazard maps, and manual/autopilot guidance.
DOMAIN 01 · INDIAN SPACE MISSIONS & SPACE DATA

Sriharikota Launch Complex & ISRO Fleet Validation

  • Default launch site at SDSC SHAR Sriharikota (13.72°N, 80.23°E) on a rotating WGS-84 ellipsoid Earth with local GMST sky alignment.
  • Fly ISRO LVM3-M4 (Chandrayaan-3) to its 170 × 36,500 km GTO against 14 July 2023 flight timestamps, plus the 2-stage ISRO RH-200 Rohini sounding rocket.

Four Integrated Modes in One 3D World

From snapping airframe tubes together to orbital staging and lunar touchdown.

MODE 01 · BUILD
40+ Parts · 8 Categories · .ork Round-Trip

3D Rocket Builder & OpenRocket CAD

  • Drag-and-drop or tap-to-snap 3D assembly across Nose Cones, Body Tubes, Transitions, Fins, Motor Mounts, Parachutes, Payloads & Rings.
  • .ork OpenRocket import and export (round-trip tested on name, stages, components, length and mass); RockSim and RASP .eng import.
  • Multi-stage decouplers, 2×/3×/4× radial strap-on boosters, cutaway internal X-ray view, and automatic Best / OK / Bad workmanship grading.
MODE 02 · LAUNCH & DIAGNOSE
2 ms RK4 · 20+ Motors · 19-Flight Sweep

6-DOF Pad Flight & Flaw Analyzer

  • Barrowman aerodynamics (normal-force slope, CP per part, pitch damping, skin/base/pressure drag + transonic wave Cd) in an ISA atmosphere to 86 km.
  • Rule-based Flaw Analyzer catches 11+ failure modes (Unstable, Over-stable, Fin Flutter, Slow Rail Exit, Hard Landing, High-g, Drift) with timestamps & fixes.
  • Interactive replay pauses at each flaw with live 3D force arrows (Thrust, Drag, Normal force at CP, Weight at CG) + 19-flight wind/rail stress sweep.
MODE 03 · ISRO MISSION
S200 + L110 + C25 · 12 Health Bars · IIP Track

LVM3-M4 Orbital Ascent & Fault Lab

  • Full 6-DOF orbital launcher from Sriharikota with closed-loop Linear-Tangent guidance, J2–J4 gravity harmonics, and vacuum-expanding two-cone exhaust plumes.
  • Live event timeline compares simulated vs actual 14 July 2023 LVM3-M4 staging (S200 sep 126.7s vs 127.0s, L110 sep 307.1s vs 305.6s).
  • Destructive Fault Injection Lab: trigger Gimbal Lock, Vikas Engine-Out, C25 Shutdown, Blocked Fairing Vent, or Tank Overpressure with live Bay of Bengal IIP impact rings.
MODE 04 · DEEP SPACE & MOON
69.37°S, 32.35°E · 3-Nested Meshes · Auto/Manual

Shiv Shakti Lunar Powered Descent

  • Deorbit from a 100 km polar lunar orbit through Braking, Approach, and Terminal descent phases to the Chandrayaan-3 Shiv Shakti landing site.
  • Multi-resolution lunar regolith heightfield (240 m rock-field, 1.6 km approach patch, full Moon globe) with real-time footpad slope & boulder hazard classification.
  • Fly hands-on with touch/keyboard pitch, roll & throttle controls or watch closed-loop PD attitude + terminal descent guidance score your touchdown.

Dual 6-DOF Physics Engines, One Unified FlightRecord Contract

Decoupling high-rate aerodynamic & orbital solvers from 60 FPS floating-origin WebGL rendering.

PRESENTATION & HUD LAYER (src/app + landing.html) · Unified .sfb-root Glass UI · Desktop & 44px Mobile Touch HUD
Mode Switcher · 3D Builder Inspector · Flight Telemetry Strip · Flaw & Health Panels · Interactive Timeline Scrubber · 1×–100,000× Time Warp
ENGINE A · DESIGN & SOUNDING SIM

Barrowman 6-DOF (src/physics)

  • Fixed 2 ms RK4 integration with quaternion attitude & dynamic CG/inertia as motors burn.
  • Per-part normal force (CNα) & CP, body lift, pitch damping, skin/base/pressure + transonic wave drag.
  • Multi-stage separation, delayed ignition, dual parachute recovery, wind gusts & 11+ flaw rules.
BRIDGE · WEB WORKER & CONTRACT

FlightRecord + ECI Adapter

  • Non-blocking Web Worker computes full orbital ascents off-thread and streams FlightRecord samples.
  • Exact orthogonal frame map: Z-up ECI (body +X nose) → Y-up App (body +Y nose) via (x, z, -y) & qApp = Cq·q·Bq.
  • Snapshot interpolator drives deterministic forward/backward scrubbing, staging & trajectory draw ranges.
ENGINE B · ORBITAL & LUNAR SIM

Orbital 6-DOF (rocket-physics + lunar)

  • WGS-84 rotating Earth, J2/J3/J4 harmonics, US Standard Atmosphere 1976 to 1000 km & Sutton-Graves heating.
  • Linear-Tangent closed-loop guidance, oblateness apogee compensation, Lambert transfer & Dormand-Prince RK5(4).
  • FaultMonitor tracks 12 structural/thermal/propulsion health channels (ullage pressure, cavitation, skin temp).
3D FLOATING-ORIGIN RENDERER (Three.js r170 · Logarithmic Depth Buffer) · Zero jitter from 1 mm rail buttons to 10⁹ km planetary orbits

• Procedural Mesh Pipeline: Exact geometry from .ork / VehicleSpec · Cutaway internals · Two-cone vacuum-expanding plumes · Booster & fairing separation kinematics

• Single TypeScript + Vite Build: Offline Service Worker PWA (dist/) + Capacitor 8 Android APK (android/) with native file export & 60 FPS WebView performance

Aerospace Educators & STEM Labs

ISRO Mission Enthusiasts
